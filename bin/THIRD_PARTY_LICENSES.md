# Third-party binaries in `bin/`

tools**, not part of Fox. They are redistributed here only for convenience in
the data-simulation pipeline. Each is the property of its authors and is
governed by its own license — **not** by Fox's MIT license.

If you redistribute this repository, you must comply with each tool's license:
keep this notice, retain the tool's license text, and make its source available
or point to it. To verify the exact version of a binary, run it with `--version`.
